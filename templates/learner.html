FAFSA (Free Application for Federal Student Aid)

Definition: The official form students must fill out to apply for federal financial aid, including grants, work-study, and loans. It's the single most important application for aid. The FAFSA is also used by many states and colleges to determine eligibility for their own aid programs.
Example: The FAFSA opens on October 1st each year. It's crucial to file it as early as possible, as some aid is first-come, first-served. You'll need your Social Security Number, tax returns, and information about your family's finances.
