💰 Types of Aid

📝 Grants

Gift Aid (Free Money) - No Repayment Required

Grants are typically awarded based on financial need. They are the best form of aid because they do not need to be repaid. The Federal Pell Grant is a common example.

🎓 Scholarships

Gift Aid (Free Money) - No Repayment Required

Scholarships are usually awarded based on merit, such as academic achievement, athletic talent, or community service. You can find them from colleges, companies, and non-profits.

💼 Federal Work-Study

Self-Help Aid - Earned, not repaid

A need-based program that provides part-time jobs for students. You earn a paycheck to help cover expenses. Money earned here isn't counted against you on next year's FAFSA.

🏦 Federal Student Loans

Self-Help Aid - Must be repaid with interest

Borrowed money that you must repay with interest. Federal loans have better benefits and lower fixed interest rates than private loans. Subsidized loans are best, as the government pays the interest while you're in school.
